Skincare Explanation

The Ordinary is a skincare brand that focuses on providing effective, affordable, and easy-to-understand skincare products. The brand is known for its minimalist approach, using carefully selected ingredients that are designed to target specific skin concerns. Dengan konsep ini, pengguna dapat lebih mudah memahami fungsi dari setiap produk dan memilih skincare yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan kulit mereka. The Ordinary offers a variety of products for different steps in a skincare routine, such as cleansers, toners, serums, moisturizers, lip care, and sun protection. Some of the ingredients commonly used include Hyaluronic Acid, Squalane, Amino Acids, Glycolic Acid, and UV filters.

Each ingredient has a different purpose, sehingga produk dapat digunakan untuk membantu menjaga kelembapan, membersihkan, merawat, dan melindungi kulit. With its simple packaging, straightforward product names, and ingredient-focused formulations, The Ordinary makes skincare feel more practical and less complicated. The products can be combined into a simple daily routine based on individual skin needs. This makes The Ordinary a suitable choice for people who want a skincare routine that is simple, clear, and focused on specific skin concerns.
